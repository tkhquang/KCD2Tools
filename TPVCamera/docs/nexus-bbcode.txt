[size=5]Foreword[/size]
A while back I made [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/1550]Third Person View (TPV Camera) Enabler[/url], which switches on the game's built-in debug third-person camera. That camera was never meant for normal play, so working around its quirks and restrictions turned into a rabbit hole, and I ended up spending far more time fixing broken things than building anything new.

This mod is a complete rewrite from the opposite direction. Instead of borrowing the debug camera, it leaves the game in its normal first-person mode and rebuilds the third-person view on top of it, then adds the parts that actually matter: simple camera collision and raycast-based crosshair alignment. It is much simpler, and it genuinely works better. The funny part is that doing it this way came together in about two weeks, against the better part of a month I spent patching the old approach.

It is still early days, so if anything feels off or you have an idea that would make it better, please let me know. I hope you enjoy it, and thank you for giving it a try.

[size=5]Installation[/size]
Everything goes in the game's binary folder, the one with [b]WHGame.dll[/b]. On Steam: [b]<KC:D 2 folder>/Bin/Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/[/b]
[list=1]
[*][b]ASI loader:[/b] KCSE loads .asi files itself, so [b]KCSE users skip this step[/b]. Everyone else: extract one x64 Ultimate ASI Loader DLL beside WHGame.dll, [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/dinput8-x64.zip]dinput8.dll[/url] (usual), or [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/version-x64.zip]version.dll[/url] / [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/winmm-x64.zip]winmm.dll[/url] if that name is taken.[/*]
[*][b]The mod:[/b] extract the archive into the same folder, with no extra subfolder. It does not use the Mods folder.[/*]
[*][b]Check:[/b] launch the game. [b]KCD2_TPVCamera.log[/b] appears beside the ASI once the mod loads.[/*]
[/list]
Coming from [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/1550]KCD2_TPVToggle[/url]? Delete KCD2_TPVToggle.asi and KCD2_TPVToggle.ini first; the two mods conflict.

[spoiler]
[b]Folder layout[/b]
[code]
Bin/Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/
|-- WHGame.dll                         (game file)
|-- dinput8.dll                        (KCSE or the ASI loader)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera.asi                 (this mod)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera.ini                 (settings)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt      (your camera rules; optional, you create it)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera_presets.json        (created on first run)
\-- KCD2_TPVCamera.log                 (created when the mod loads)
[/code]

[b]No log file?[/b] Check that the ASI and the loader DLL sit directly beside WHGame.dll, and that the loader is the x64 build. With KCSE, update KCSE to its latest version. Without it, try another loader name. Never overwrite a DLL that belongs to another mod.

[b]Linux / Steam Deck:[/b] add this to the game's Steam launch options, using your loader's name (version or winmm) if it is not dinput8:
[code]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dinput8=n,b" %command%[/code]

[b]Updating:[/b] back up your INI, extract the new archive, then copy your settings back in. Keep KCD2_TPVCamera_presets.json and KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt; updates never touch them.

[b]Uninstall:[/b] delete every file starting with KCD2_TPVCamera beside WHGame.dll, and the loader DLL if no other mod needs it. Optionally, delete the files starting with kcd2_tpvcamera_ in the game's shader cache ([b]%USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\kingdomcome2\shaders\cache\d3d12\[/b] and its cg...shaders subfolders); leave the game's own files there.
[/spoiler]

[size=5]Configuration[/size]
Camera framing lives in the in-game preset manager (Home) and is saved to KCD2_TPVCamera_presets.json. Everything else is in [b]KCD2_TPVCamera.ini[/b], where every option is documented. Most changes apply as soon as you save.

[spoiler]
[list]
[*][b][Settings][/b] - log level, view hotkeys, auto-enable at start (AutoEnableTPV)[/*]
[*][b][Camera][/b] - zoom and shoulder keys, screen effects hidden per view (HideScreenEffectsTPV, HideScreenEffectsFPV), view transition, camera stability, native turn animation[/*]
[*][b][Orbit][/b] - free-look keys and cursor freeze[/*]
[*][b][Collision][/b] - prop fading (UseOccluderFade), foliage window (UseTreeSeeThrough), lens cut (UseLensCut), character fade (CloseUpFade)[/*]
[*][b][StateBehavior][/b] - which situations switch the view or pause free-look[/*]
[*][b][Ranged][/b] - aim at the crosshair, landing preview, projectile trail, drop per weapon[/*]
[*][b][Presets][/b] - preset cycle keys[/*]
[*][b][Advanced][/b] - ExportRules and ExportSignatures[/*]
[/list]
[/spoiler]

[size=5]Camera Rules[/size]
Camera rules decide what happens with each object: stop the camera, fade, open in the see-through window, or be left alone. Defaults are built into the mod. Put your own changes in [b]KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt[/b] beside the ASI; they apply while the game runs and updates never touch them.

[spoiler]
[b]One rule per line:[/b] [code]<match> = <behaviour> [options][/code]
A match is a folder, one model, a text (*hlod*), a state (@actor, @animated, @nodraw, @merged, @unnamed) or @default. Behaviours: collide, fade, tree, bush, grass, frame, pass, character, exclude.

[b]Examples:[/b]
[code]
objects/manmade/vehicles/wagons/wagon_c_hay.cgf = fade                 # the covered hay wagon fades
objects/manmade/vehicles/wagons/ = fade                                # every covered wagon fades
objects/manmade/structures/industrial/hay/ = collide mesh              # all hay stops the camera
objects/manmade/common_furniture/beds/bed_warmer_fancy.cgf = exclude   # one model left out
objects/mymod/trees/ = tree                                            # a mod's trees open in the window
[/code]

[b]Find a model's path:[/b] set LogLevel = TRACE, walk up to the object, and the log names it with the rule that decided it:
[code]Rules: objects/manmade/vehicles/wagons/wagon_c_hay.cgf [brush] -> collide mesh (built-in KCD2_TPVCamera_rules.txt:342 '...')[/code]

[b]See the built-in rules:[/b] set ExportRules = true in [Advanced]. The mod writes them, with notes on the syntax, to KCD2_TPVCamera_rules.default.txt.

[b]Mod authors:[/b] ship KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_<YourMod>.txt as a separate optional file the player copies beside the ASI (rules are not read from the Mods folder), and keep folder lines to your own folders.

Full syntax, which rule wins and what rules cannot change: [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/blob/main/TPVCamera/README.md#camera-rules]README[/url].
[/spoiler]

[size=5]Known Limitations[/size]
[list]
[*]Free-look orbit is a rough proof of concept, and it turns off on horseback and in combat.[/*]
[*]Some interactions can still be janky in third person; toggle the camera off if one looks wrong.[/*]
[/list]

[spoiler]
[list]
[*]Free-look orbit also pauses during the focused look (Left Alt, or LB on a controller).[/*]
[*]The first-person body can look slightly off from behind in some animations.[/*]
[*]Faded objects use the engine's dither, so a large faded cart shows a fine screen-door pattern.[/*]
[*]The foliage window, the lens cut and Henry's close-up fade use shaders prepared at loading screens and pauses (Esc). A plant first seen during play keeps its normal look until then, and loading can take longer.[/*]
[*]A held torch's flame and light do not fade with Henry.[/*]
[*]In busy towns the fades cost about 0.1 ms more per frame. To skip them, set UseOccluderFade, UseTreeSeeThrough, UseLensCut and CloseUpFade to false and CollisionPullInSeconds to 0 in [Collision].[/*]
[*]The crosshair fix does not cover every interaction type yet.[/*]
[*]A shot at a very close target leaves the bow at a visible angle to reach the crosshair. Lower MaxAimCorrection in [Ranged] to keep the game's own shot there.[/*]
[*]A side offset above about 1 m can put the crosshair out of Henry's reach, so the use prompt does not show.[/*]
[*]The preset manager costs some frame rate while open. It avoids the game's swap chain on purpose, to stay compatible with ReShade and OptiScaler.[/*]
[/list]
[/spoiler]
