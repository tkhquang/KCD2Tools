[size=5]Foreword[/size]
A while back I made [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/1550]Third Person View (TPV Camera) Enabler[/url], which switches on the game's built-in debug third-person camera. That camera was never meant for normal play, so working around its quirks and restrictions turned into a rabbit hole, and I ended up spending far more time fixing broken things than building anything new.

This mod is a complete rewrite from the opposite direction. Instead of borrowing the debug camera, it leaves the game in its normal first-person mode and rebuilds the third-person view on top of it, then adds the parts that actually matter: simple camera collision and raycast-based crosshair alignment. It is much simpler, and it genuinely works better. The funny part is that doing it this way came together in about two weeks, against the better part of a month I spent patching the old approach.

It is still early days, so if anything feels off or you have an idea that would make it better, please let me know. I hope you enjoy it, and thank you for giving it a try.

[size=5]Installation[/size]
This is an [b].asi[/b] plugin and requires an [b]ASI loader[/b], which is [b]not bundled[/b] in the download.

[size=4]Find the installation folder[/size]
Open your KC:D 2 installation folder, then open the Bin/Win64MasterMaster... subfolder that contains [b]WHGame.dll[/b]. On Steam this is:
[code]<KC:D 2 installation folder>/Bin/Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/[/code]
Other stores use their own Bin/Win64MasterMaster... folder; use WHGame.dll to identify the correct one. The loader and mod files go directly into this folder.

If you have the older [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/1550]KCD2_TPVToggle[/url] mod installed, remove KCD2_TPVToggle.asi and KCD2_TPVToggle.ini first. TPVCamera replaces it, and running both camera mods at once will conflict. To keep a backup, move the old ASI outside the game folder or rename its extension to .asi.bak.

[size=4]Step 1: Install an ASI loader[/size]
If you already have a working ASI loader for KC:D 2, skip to Step 2. The same loader can load TPVCamera, Henry's Senses and other ASI mods.

Download [b]one x64[/b] variant of [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases]Ultimate ASI Loader[/url] by ThirteenAG:
[list]
[*][url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/dinput8-x64.zip][b]dinput8.dll[/b][/url] - Usual choice if dinput8.dll is unused.[/*]
[*][url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/version-x64.zip][b]version.dll[/b][/url] - Alternative when dinput8.dll is already used, including by KCSE.[/*]
[*][url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/winmm-x64.zip][b]winmm.dll[/b][/url] - Another alternative if version.dll is also used or does not load.[/*]
[/list]
Extract the DLL from the chosen ZIP directly beside WHGame.dll. Use the [b]x64[/b] build, not Win32, and install only one Ultimate ASI Loader variant. Do not overwrite a DLL belonging to another mod.

[b]Using [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3332]Kingdom Come Script Extender (KCSE)[/url]?[/b] KCSE uses [b]dinput8.dll[/b]. Keep KCSE's file and install Ultimate ASI Loader as [b]version.dll[/b] or [b]winmm.dll[/b] in the same folder. Choose an unused name from the downloads above; do not rename or replace KCSE's DLL.

[size=4]Step 2: Install the mod[/size]
Download the TPVCamera release archive from the Files tab or [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/releases]GitHub Releases[/url]. Extract its contents directly into the [b]same folder[/b] beside WHGame.dll and your loader DLL, without an extra archive-named subfolder.
[list]
[*][b]KCD2_TPVCamera.asi[/b] - The mod.[/*]
[*][b]KCD2_TPVCamera.ini[/b] - Hotkeys and global settings.[/*]
[*]The camera rules, which set how the camera treats each object in the world, are built into the ASI. Your own changes go in [b]KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt[/b], which you create (see Camera Rules below).[/*]
[*][b]KCD2_TPVCamera_presets.json[/b] - Created on first run; not included in the archive.[/*]
[/list]
TPVCamera does not use the Mods folder.

[size=4]Step 3: Launch and verify[/size]
Launch the game; third-person turns on automatically once you reach gameplay. Press F3 (or hold LB + RB on a controller) to toggle back to first-person.

[b]How to verify the mod loaded:[/b] check for [b]KCD2_TPVCamera.log[/b] beside the ASI. If it is missing, check that the ASI and x64 loader DLL are directly beside WHGame.dll, then try another unused loader variant and relaunch. Replace only the Ultimate ASI Loader DLL you installed; keep KCSE and other mods' DLLs. On Wine/Proton, also check the override below.

When updating, back up your customized INI before extracting the new archive, then reapply your settings to the supplied INI. Keep KCD2_TPVCamera_presets.json to retain your saved camera presets. An update brings its camera rules built into the ASI and never touches your own KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt. If you replaced the built-in rules with a KCD2_TPVCamera_rules.txt of your own, that file stays in force, and the rules of an update reach you only once you delete it.

[b]Folder layout[/b] (everything goes in the binary folder, next to WHGame.dll):
[code]
Bin/Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/
|-- WHGame.dll                         (game file, already present)
|-- dinput8.dll                        (ASI loader; see KCSE note above)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera.asi                  (this mod)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera.ini                  (settings)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt       (your own camera rules; optional, you create it)
|-- KCD2_TPVCamera_presets.json         (created on first run)
\-- KCD2_TPVCamera.log                  (created when the mod loads)
[/code]
With KCSE, dinput8.dll in this example belongs to KCSE; your chosen ASI loader, version.dll or winmm.dll, sits alongside it.

[size=5]Linux / Steam Deck (Wine/Proton)[/size]
Add an override for your chosen loader DLL in the game's [b]Properties -> Launch Options[/b] on Steam:
[code]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dinput8=n,b" %command%[/code]
Use version=n,b or winmm=n,b instead when using that loader name. If you also use KCSE's dinput8.dll with the version.dll loader, retain both overrides:
[code]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dinput8,version=n,b" %command%[/code]
For a command-line launch, set the same WINEDLLOVERRIDES value before your launch command. Keep any overrides your other mods need.

[size=5]Uninstall[/size]
[list]
[*]In the folder beside WHGame.dll, delete every file whose name starts with [b]KCD2_TPVCamera[/b]: the ASI, the INI, the rules files (your own, other mods' patches and an exported copy included), the presets, the logs and any signature files. Delete the ASI loader DLL only if no other mod uses it.[/*]
[*]Optionally, delete the shaders TPVCamera wrote to the game's shader cache, usually [b]%USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\kingdomcome2\shaders\cache\d3d12\[/b] (the log line "ShaderTwins: ... twin file(s) in <folder>" names it): the files whose names start with [b]kcd2_tpvcamera_[/b], in that folder and in its cg...shaders subfolders. Leave the rest of the folder alone: it is the game's own shader cache, and deleting it only makes the game compile its shaders again.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Default Controls[/size]
All keys are rebindable in KCD2_TPVCamera.ini.
[list]
[*][b]Enter / exit third-person:[/b] F3, or hold LB + RB[/*]
[*][b]Toggle free-look orbit:[/b] F4, or hold LB + click the left stick (LS)[/*]
[*][b]Hold for free-look orbit:[/b] unbound by default (momentary freelook: hold to look around, release to snap back)[/*]
[*][b]Open preset manager overlay:[/b] Home[/*]
[*][b]Zoom in:[/b] LShift + PageUp, or hold LB + D-pad up[/*]
[*][b]Zoom out:[/b] LShift + PageDown, or hold LB + D-pad down[/*]
[*][b]Force first-person / force third-person:[/b] unbound by default[/*]
[*][b]Cycle to next / previous preset:[/b] unbound by default (for example Numpad6 / Numpad4, or hold LB + D-pad right / left)[/*]
[*][b]Swap shoulder:[/b] unbound by default (SwapShoulderKey)[/*]
[/list]
On a controller the zoom shares the D-pad with the game's inventory/map shortcut, so the mod hides that D-pad button from the game while you zoom (the ZoomInKey.Consume / ZoomOutKey.Consume keys, on by default) so finishing a zoom does not open the inventory or map. Set them to false if you would rather the D-pad keep reaching the game. The keyboard zoom keys are unaffected.

[size=5]Configuration[/size]
Edit KCD2_TPVCamera.ini, grouped into:
[list]
[*][b][Settings][/b] - log level, the view hotkeys, the preset-overlay key, and the start-of-session auto-enable flags[/*]
[*][b][Camera][/b] - zoom keys, the shoulder swap key (SwapShoulderKey) and whether the swap is saved (AutoSaveShoulder), the camera-space interaction toggle, the view-transition ease, the camera-stability options (StableAimBasis, AimBasisSmoothing) that keep the view steady against head-bob, sway, and combat shake, and the native turn animation (NativeTurnAnimation, with NativeTurnAngle and NativeTurnSettleDelay tuning when Henry turns on the spot to face where you look). The framing itself is per-preset[/*]
[*][b][Orbit][/b] - the free-look orbit keys (press-to-toggle and momentary hold) and the cursor freeze (the orbit feel is per-preset)[/*]
[*][b][Collision][/b] - the collision probe and radius, UseOccluderFade (fade the props that hide your character instead of pulling in; buildings still stop the camera) with how much of them stays and which props count as in front of your character (enable, skin, return speed and shoulder collision are per-preset), UseTreeSeeThrough (open a dithered window around your character in the trees, bushes and tall grass in front of it) with how much of the plants stays, its size, where it starts up the body and its softness, UseLensCut (cut away what comes right up to the lens), HeadClearance, the room the camera needs behind your character, and CloseUpFade (fade the character and everything it wears and holds instead of switching to first person when there is less) with how much of the character always stays[/*]
[*][b][StateBehavior][/b] - switch first/third person on entering a situation (combat, aiming, dialogue, minigame, mount, menu, overlay), restore the prior view on exit (manual toggles during it stick), suspend/restore free-look in chosen situations, choose where the native turn animation is switched off (NativeTurnExcludeState), and whether loading a crossbow already counts as aiming (ReloadCountsAsAiming)[/*]
[*][b][Ranged][/b] - aim third-person shots (arrows, bolts, handgonne balls) and distraction stones at the crosshair (AimAtCrosshair), the largest correction allowed (MaxAimCorrection), the flight trail (ShowProjectileTrail, ProjectileTrailSeconds), a live preview of where a drawn shot or a held distraction stone will land (ShowAimPreview, AimPreviewArc, AimPreviewOpacity, AimPreviewColor, AimPreviewThroughWalls), and how much your shots drop per weapon (BowGravity, CrossbowGravity, FirearmGravity)[/*]
[*][b][Presets][/b] - the keys that cycle presets bound to the same situation (NextPresetKey, PreviousPresetKey) and whether the choice is saved (AutoSaveCyclePick); the camera framing lives in the in-game preset manager (open with Home) and is stored in KCD2_TPVCamera_presets.json next to the INI. That file is created automatically from the built-in defaults on first run; it is not shipped, so updating the mod never overwrites presets you have tuned[/*]
[*][b][Advanced][/b] - the self-heal window, ExportSignatures (writes the built-in signatures for reporting a problem after a game update) and ExportRules (see Camera Rules below)[/*]
[/list]
Most values apply the next frame after you save the file.

How the camera treats each object (which ones stop it, fade, let it through, open in the see-through window or thin away at the lens) is set by the camera rules, not in the INI; see Camera Rules below. UseOccluderFade and UseTreeSeeThrough still turn the fade and the window on or off for every object, whatever the rules say.

[size=5]Camera Rules[/size]
The camera rules decide how the camera treats every object in the world: which ones stop it like a wall, fade while they hide your character, let it through, open in the see-through window or thin away at the lens. They go by the model's path in the game files, for a whole folder or one model, so you can change any of them and cover other mods' models too. The [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/blob/main/TPVCamera/README.md#camera-rules]README[/url] has the full syntax, which rule wins and what the rules cannot change.
[list]
[*][b]Built-in rules[/b] - built into the ASI and updated with it. Set ExportRules = true in the INI's [Advanced] section to write them, with notes that explain the syntax, to KCD2_TPVCamera_rules.default.txt (TPVCamera never reads that file).[/*]
[*][b]KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_<Name>.txt[/b] - patches from other mods or from you, read next in name order.[/*]
[*][b]KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt[/b] - your own changes, read last, so they win. Updates never touch it.[/*]
[/list]
Every rules file sits beside KCD2_TPVCamera.asi. Edits apply while the game runs, about a second after you save. To replace the built-in rules entirely, rename the exported file to KCD2_TPVCamera_rules.txt; the rules of later updates then reach you only once you delete it.

[b]One rule per line:[/b] [code]<match> = <behaviour> [options][/code]
A match is a folder, one model, a text (*hlod*), a state (@actor, @animated, @nodraw, @merged, @unnamed) or @default. The behaviours are collide, fade, tree, bush, grass, frame, pass, character and exclude.

[b]Find a model's path:[/b] set LogLevel = TRACE, go up to the object, and the log names it with the rule that decided it:
[code]Rules: objects/manmade/vehicles/wagons/wagon_c_hay.cgf [brush] -> collide mesh (built-in KCD2_TPVCamera_rules.txt:342 'objects/manmade/vehicles/wagons/wagon_c_hay.cgf')[/code]

[b]Examples[/b] for KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_User.txt:
[code]
objects/manmade/vehicles/wagons/wagon_c_hay.cgf = fade                         # the covered hay wagon fades
objects/manmade/vehicles/wagons/ = fade                                        # every covered wagon fades
objects/manmade/structures/industrial/hay/ = collide mesh                      # all hay stops the camera
objects/manmade/common_furniture/beds/bed_warmer_fancy.cgf = exclude           # one model left out of the mod
objects/mymod/trees/ = tree                                                    # a mod's trees open in the window
[/code]

[b]For mod authors:[/b] ship your rules as KCD2_TPVCamera_rules_<YourMod>.txt, a separate optional file the player copies beside KCD2_TPVCamera.asi by hand (TPVCamera reads no rules from the Mods folder), and keep folder lines to your mod's own folders. See the README for details.

[size=5]Using with Controllers[/size]
DetourModKit supports gamepad input natively via the [b]XInput[/b] API. Use gamepad button names directly in the INI:

[code]
; Toggle with the gamepad Y button
ToggleViewKey = Gamepad_Y

; Use a modifier combo (hold LB + press RB)
ToggleViewKey = Gamepad_LB+Gamepad_RB

; Multiple independent combos (comma = OR)
ToggleViewKey = F3,Gamepad_LB+Gamepad_RB
[/code]

[b]XInput only:[/b] Xbox controllers work natively. For PS4/PS5/Switch controllers, use an XInput translation layer (e.g. DS4Windows, DualSenseX, BetterJoy) or Steam Input to present your controller as XInput. See [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit?tab=readme-ov-file#gamepad-compatibility]Gamepad Compatibility[/url] for details.

[size=5]Known Limitations[/size]
[list]
[*]Free-look orbit is a raw proof-of-concept: fine for swinging the camera around to look at the front of Henry, but rough for normal gameplay.[/*]
[*]Free-look orbit conflicts with the game's own camera control on horseback and in combat, so it is auto-disabled in those situations.[/*]
[*]The first-person body rig can still look slightly off from behind in some animations.[/*]
[*]Faded objects use the engine's dither, so a large faded cart or ladder wagon shows a fine screen-door pattern rather than smooth transparency.[/*]
[*]The plant window, the cut of tent frames at the lens and the fade of your character's eyes, hair and beard use copies of the game's shaders, prepared at the loading screen or the next pause (the Esc menu, or a menu screen while the game is paused). A plant first seen during play keeps its normal look until then, and a close-up before the character's copies are ready hides the head, as in first person. Loading may take longer.[/*]
[*]A held torch's flame and light do not fade with your character.[/*]
[*]In busy towns the fades, the see-through window and the lens cut take the camera about 0.3 ms per frame, against about 0.2 ms without them. For the lowest cost, set UseOccluderFade, UseTreeSeeThrough, UseLensCut and CloseUpFade to false and CollisionPullInSeconds to 0 in [Collision]: the camera then stops at what it hits, as in 2.0.0 (with this version's fixes), prepares no shaders at loading screens or pauses, and costs about what 2.0.0 did.[/*]
[*]The raycast-based crosshair fix is partial: it does not cover every interaction type, so some still show parallax or an offset.[/*]
[*]A third-person shot at a close target, such as the ground at Henry's feet, is turned up to about 50 degrees from where the bow points, so the arrow visibly leaves the bow at an angle before it hits the crosshair. A target behind the bow cannot be reached that way and takes the game's own shot. Lower MaxAimCorrection in [Ranged] (90 degrees by default) to keep the game's shot for close targets instead.[/*]
[*]A large Offset Right (above about 1 m) can put the crosshair out of Henry's reach. The game only offers herbs within about 1.5 m of his eyes and doors, beds and other usables within about 1 m to his side, so the use prompt does not show for what the crosshair rests on. Lower the offset, or swing the crosshair back over something close to Henry.[/*]
[*]Certain world interactions in third person can still be janky or buggy; toggle the camera off if one looks wrong.[/*]
[*]The preset overlay does not hook the game's swap chain by design: it renders through a private WARP device and composites with a GDI blit, which keeps it compatible with overlays and injectors like ReShade and OptiScaler. The trade-off is a frame-rate hit while the overlay is open; since it is a tuning panel you open briefly rather than play with, that is acceptable.[/*]
[/list]
