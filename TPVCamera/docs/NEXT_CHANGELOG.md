## Smoother Camera Collision and Close-Up Fade

- Added the fade of everything Henry wears and holds to the close-up fade: the weapons in his hands and on his belt and back, a drawn bow and the arrow on it, a crossbow, the quiver and the shield.
- Added the fade of his eyes, eyelashes, hair, beard and the thin wet film over his eyes, so his head stays shown instead of being switched off. The game prepares the shaders they fade with in the background while you play in third person, once after you install or update the mod (up to about a minute of play), so the first close-up normally finds them ready. A close-up before that hides his head instead.
- Changed the close-up camera: when there is no room behind Henry (a wall right behind, a low doorway), Henry fades out instead of the view jumping to first person. He never disappears completely: a quarter of him stays by default (CloseUpFadeMinOpacity). His shadow stays. CloseUpFade turns this off.
- Fixed the camera jiggling when you swing it up and down next to a market stall, a roof edge or a counter.
