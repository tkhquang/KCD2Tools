# Changelog

All notable changes to the TrueLight mod will be documented in this file.

## [1.1.0] - Torch Brightness Setting and Hidden Torch Fix

- New `true_light_bulb_size` setting: a smaller value makes Henry's torch dimmer and his shadow lighter (`0.3` matches an NPC's torch, the default `1` keeps the game's brightness).
- The scene no longer goes dark while another mod hides the torch during an animation.

## [1.0.0] - Initial Release

- Henry's torch lights the scene from its flame, with shadows in every direction, his own included, through any animation.
- Lanterns from mods too; other mods' handheld lights can be added in `mod.cfg`.
- Crouching and mounting keep the light on, from just above Henry's head.
- Third person, photo mode and first person can each be turned on or off in `mod.cfg`. Works with TPVCamera or any camera mod.
