[size=5]Foreword[/size]
Three cutscenes made with the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3981]Animation Framework[/url] and played through the game's own cutscene player. Talk to Magdalena at the forge in Kutna Hora and pick one.

[b]A showcase and a set of examples for modders who want to make their own cutscenes.[/b] It adds no gameplay.

[size=5]Key Features[/size]
[list]
[*][b]A dance battle:[/b] Henry challenges Magdalena. Both dance, and he wins or loses at random, in front of a crowd that cheers, claps and laughs.[/*]
[*][b]A game cutscene, recast:[/b] the fencers' bout from Kutna Hora, in its arena, with Henry, Jobst von Bergow and Hans in other people's places.[/*]
[*][b]The same cutscene, moved:[/b] the bout in an open field as a duel between Henry and Hans, with new camera work, grass footsteps and its music.[/*]
[*][b]Real voices:[/b] Henry and Magdalena speak with their own recorded lines from the game.[/*]
[*][b]Made to learn from:[/b] every file is open, and the [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/blob/main/CutsceneExamples/docs/DEVLOG.md]devlog[/url] shows how each scene was made.[/*]
[*][url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/tree/main/CutsceneExamples]Open source[/url], built on [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3981]Animation Framework[/url] (built with [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit]DetourModKit[/url])[/*]
[/list]
[color=red][b]Spoiler: both fight cutscenes come from the side quest Ars Dimicatoria (the Kuttenberg swordfighters). If you haven't finished it, consider not watching them. It's just the fight, with the fighters' models swapped, but now you know.[/b][/color]

[size=5]Requirements[/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3981]Animation Framework[/url] 1.2.0 or newer[/*]
[*]The Legacy of the Forge DLC (Magdalena is from it)[/*]
[*]Optional: [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3860]Sound Framework[/url] 1.3.0 or newer, for the dialog voices. Without it the lines show as subtitles only.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
Install with your mod manager, or extract into your [b]Mods[/b] folder. To uninstall, delete [b]Mods/cutscene_examples[/b].
[spoiler]
[code]
Mods/
\-- cutscene_examples/
    |-- mod.manifest
    |-- Data/
    |   |-- cutscene_examples.pak
    |   \-- Levels/kutnohorsko/cutscene_examples.pak
    |-- Localization/
    |   \-- English_xml.pak
    \-- SoundFramework/
        \-- cutscene_examples.ini
[/code]
[/spoiler]

[size=5]Playing the examples[/size]
Talk to Magdalena and pick a topic. The cutscene starts when the conversation closes. Press Space twice to skip it.
[list]
[*][b]"I challenge you to a (dance) duel!"[/b] plays the dance battle in the forge yard.[/*]
[*][b]"Did I ever tell you how I beat von Bergow at fencing?"[/b] plays the fencers' bout where the game plays it.[/*]
[*][b]"Hans and I had a friendly bout."[/b] plays the bout in an open field west of Kutna Hora.[/*]
[/list]
[color=red][b]The last two are from the side quest Ars Dimicatoria. See the spoiler note above.[/b][/color]
