# Changelog

All notable changes to the CutsceneExamples mod will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - Initial Release

- Talk to Magdalena at the forge in Kutna Hora and pick one of three cutscenes.
- A dance battle: Henry challenges Magdalena and wins or loses at random, in front of a cheering, laughing crowd.
- The fencers' bout from Kutna Hora, copied from the game, with Henry, Jobst von Bergow and Hans in other people's places.
- The same bout in an open field, as a duel between Henry and Hans, with new camera work, grass footsteps and its music.
- Henry and Magdalena speak with their own recorded lines from the game (needs Sound Framework).
- Fades, sound, skipping and an end card on a black screen, like the game's own cutscenes.
