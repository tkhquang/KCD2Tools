## Initial Release

- Hold G (or RB and Y on a controller), point at a gesture and let go: Henry plays it and says a line.
- Eight gestures ready to use: wave, sword pose, play the flute, sit on the ground, read, laugh, rude and threaten.
- The person in front of Henry reacts: they nod or refuse, answer, like him more or less, and may attack after repeated threats.
- Edit any slot in game with any animation, voice line, reaction and item to hold from the game.
- Gestures work seated and on horseback, and a torch's light stays on while it is hidden.
- Moving ends a gesture; a gesture can be locked so moving does not end it.
- Other mods can add their own gestures and reactions.
