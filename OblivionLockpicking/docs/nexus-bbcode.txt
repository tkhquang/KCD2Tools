[size=5]Foreword[/size]
My shaky hands never got along with the vanilla lockpicking, so I set out to replace it. I have to confess I spent more than a year on this mod and on [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3860]Sound Framework[/url], which I built for it.

[b]Oblivion-Style Lockpicking - Tumbler Lock[/b] replaces the lockpicking minigame with a tumbler lock in the style of [i]The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion[/i]. The mechanics are recreated from observing how Oblivion's lockpicking plays. It is not a port and uses no Oblivion code, art or sound: the lock art is made for this mod, and the sounds are the game's own.

[youtube]dSIp7Ti-nFA[/youtube]

[size=5]How It Works[/size]
[list]
[*]A lock has one to five tumblers, more on harder locks.[/*]
[*]Push a tumbler up with the pick. It rises at one of three speeds, pauses at the top, then falls back.[/*]
[*]Set it while it pauses. A set at any other moment is a wrong set: the game rolls whether the pick breaks, and the controls pause for a moment.[/*]
[*]A wrong set keeps some of your set tumblers, more with a higher Thievery level. Set them all and the lock opens.[/*]
[*]Thievery, perks and buffs slow the tumblers down. Experience, noise, crime and broken picks stay the game's own.[/*]
[/list]
[spoiler]
[list]
[*]The lock is 15th-century Gothic ironwork, rusty on common locks and gilt on fancy ones, and richer on harder locks. The pick is shaped like the game's lockpick.[/*]
[*]Every push, click, fall and set has its own sound through Sound Framework.[/*]
[*]Keyboard, mouse and controller, with Oblivion Remastered's controls by default and every key rebindable. All texts are in the game's 16 languages.[/*]
[*]Settings apply while the game runs, and Enabled=false brings the vanilla minigame back.[/*]
[/list]
[/spoiler]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
1. Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3860]Sound Framework[/url] first, as its page describes. Either of its builds works with either build of this mod.
2. The Files tab has two builds of this mod. [b]Install one of them, not both.[/b]

[b]KCSE build:[/b] install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3332]KCSE[/url], then install the (KCSE) archive with your mod manager, or extract it into <KC:D 2 folder>/Mods/:
[code]
Mods/
\-- oblivion_lockpicking/
    |-- mod.manifest
    |-- Data/
    |   \-- oblivion_lockpicking.pak               (the lock's art and movie)
    |-- Localization/
    |   \-- English_xml.pak, French_xml.pak, ...   (texts, one pak per language)
    |-- SoundFramework/
    |   \-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking_sounds.ini    (the lock's sounds, read by Sound Framework)
    \-- KCSE/
        \-- Plugins/
            |-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.dll       (this mod)
            |-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.ini       (settings)
            |-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking_License.txt
            \-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.log       (created when the mod loads)
[/code]

[b]ASI build[/b]
[spoiler]
1. Without an ASI loader, extract one [b]x64[/b] [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases]Ultimate ASI Loader[/url] DLL beside WHGame.dll in Bin/Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/. Other stores use their own Bin/Win64MasterMaster... folder. Use [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/dinput8-x64.zip]dinput8-x64.zip[/url], or [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/version-x64.zip]version-x64.zip[/url] when another mod already uses dinput8.dll. KCSE also loads ASI files, so KCSE users skip this step.
2. Extract the (ASI) archive into the game folder, so its Bin and Mods folders merge with the game's. Without Mods/oblivion_lockpicking/, the game keeps its vanilla lockpicking.
3. On Linux or Steam Deck, add [b]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dinput8=n,b" %command%[/b] to the game's Steam launch options (version=n,b for the version loader).
[code]
<KC:D 2 folder>/
|-- Bin/
|   \-- Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/
|       |-- WHGame.dll                             (game file, already present)
|       |-- dinput8.dll                            (ASI loader)
|       |-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.asi           (this mod)
|       |-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.ini           (settings)
|       |-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking_License.txt
|       \-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.log           (created when the mod loads)
\-- Mods/
    \-- oblivion_lockpicking/
        |-- mod.manifest
        |-- Data/
        |   \-- oblivion_lockpicking.pak           (the lock's art and movie)
        |-- Localization/
        |   \-- English_xml.pak, French_xml.pak, ... (texts, one pak per language)
        \-- SoundFramework/
            \-- KCD2_OblivionLockpicking_sounds.ini (the lock's sounds, read by Sound Framework)
[/code]
[/spoiler]

Recommended: [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/654]FPS FIX (Memory Leak)[/url] and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3462]Map Close Crash Bugfix[/url], which fix crashes in the game's own memory handling that a UI-heavy mod can also run into.

[size=5]Controls[/size]
[list]
[*][b]Move the pick:[/b] A / D or the mouse, or the left stick[/*]
[*][b]Push the tumbler:[/b] W or move the mouse up, or push the left stick up[/*]
[*][b]Set the tumbler at the top:[/b] Space or left click, or A / Cross[/*]
[/list]
Hold the push to keep a tumbler bouncing. KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.ini documents every setting, and [Controls] changes any key.

[size=5]Troubleshooting[/size]
Set LogLevel=DEBUG in the INI, reproduce the problem, and [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/issues]report it[/url] with KCD2_OblivionLockpicking.log, your INI, and your game and mod versions. The log is beside the ASI, or in Mods/oblivion_lockpicking/KCSE/Plugins/ for the KCSE build (with MO2, in its overwrite folder).
