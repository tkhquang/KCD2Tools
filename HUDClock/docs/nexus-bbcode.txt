[size=5]Foreword[/size]
[b]HUD Clock[/b] shows the game time on the HUD.

[size=5]Features[/size]
[list]
[*]Shows with the HUD and in minigames (alchemy, smithing, dice, reading), optionally in photo mode. Hides in menus, dialogue and cutscenes.[/*]
[*]Fully customizable: 24 or 12 hours, part of the day, day count, real time, sun and moon dial, fonts, colors, position, and when it shows. See KCD2_HUDClock.ini for more.[/*]
[*]Open source, built with [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit]DetourModKit[/url].[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Requirements[/size]
[list]
[*]Kingdom Come: Deliverance II 1.5.6 on Steam.[/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3332]Kingdom Come Script Extender (KCSE)[/url], or an ASI loader for the ASI add-on.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
[b]Main file:[/b] install it with your mod manager, or extract it into your [b]Mods[/b] folder. With KCSE, that is all.
[spoiler]
[code]
<KC:D 2 folder>/
|-- Bin/
|   \-- Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/
|       |-- KingdomCome.exe              (game file)
|       \-- WHGame.dll                   (game file)
\-- Mods/
    \-- hud_clock/
        |-- mod.manifest
        |-- Data/
        |   \-- hud_clock.pak            (the clock's movie)
        \-- KCSE/
            \-- Plugins/
                |-- KCD2_HUDClock.dll    (this mod)
                |-- KCD2_HUDClock.ini    (settings)
                |-- KCD2_HUDClock_License.txt
                \-- KCD2_HUDClock.log    (created when the mod loads)
[/code]
[/spoiler]

[b]Without KCSE:[/b] install the main file too. Without an ASI loader, extract one x64 [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases]Ultimate ASI Loader[/url] DLL beside WHGame.dll. Then extract the optional [b]ASI add-on[/b] into the game folder. The ASI reads the KCD2_HUDClock.ini beside it.
[spoiler]
[code]
<KC:D 2 folder>/
|-- Bin/
|   \-- Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/
|       |-- KingdomCome.exe              (game file)
|       |-- WHGame.dll                   (game file)
|       |-- dinput8.dll                  (ASI loader)
|       |-- KCD2_HUDClock.asi            (this mod)
|       |-- KCD2_HUDClock.ini            (settings)
|       |-- KCD2_HUDClock_License.txt
|       \-- KCD2_HUDClock.log            (created when the mod loads)
\-- Mods/
    \-- hud_clock/                       (main file)
[/code]
On Linux or Steam Deck, add [b]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dinput8=n,b" %command%[/b] to the Steam launch options.
[/spoiler]

[b]KCD2_HUDClock.log[/b] appears beside the INI once the mod runs. With both KCSE and the add-on installed, only one copy runs. To uninstall, delete Mods/hud_clock and the add-on's files.

[size=5]Settings[/size]
[b]KCD2_HUDClock.ini[/b] explains every setting, and changes apply while the game runs.

[b]ToggleKey[/b] and [b]ShowKey[/b] are empty by default. They take named keys such as F7, Numpad1, Mouse4 or Gamepad_A, combos such as LShift+T, and alternatives such as F7,Gamepad_LB+Gamepad_Y. See the [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit?tab=readme-ov-file#supported-input-names]Supported Input Names[/url] reference. Controllers are XInput only, see [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit?tab=readme-ov-file#gamepad-compatibility]Gamepad Compatibility[/url].

[size=5]Compatibility[/size]
[list]
[*]Replaces no game files and binds no keys by default, so keybind, compass and other HUD mods keep working beside it.[/*]
[/list]
