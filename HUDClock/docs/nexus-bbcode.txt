[size=5]Foreword[/size]
[b]HUD Clock[/b] shows the game time on the HUD.

[size=5]Features[/size]
[list]
[*]Shows with the HUD and in minigames (alchemy, smithing, dice, reading), optionally in photo mode. Hides in menus, dialogue and cutscenes.[/*]
[*]Fully customizable: 24 or 12 hours, part of the day, day count, real time, sun and moon dial, fonts, colors, position, and when it shows. See KCD2_HUDClock.ini for more.[/*]
[*]Open source, built with [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit]DetourModKit[/url].[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Requirements[/size]
[list]
[*]Kingdom Come: Deliverance II 1.5.6 on Steam.[/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3332]Kingdom Come Script Extender (KCSE)[/url], or an ASI loader with the ASI add-on.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
Install the main file with your mod manager, or extract it into your [b]Mods[/b] folder. With KCSE, that is all.
[spoiler]
[code]
Mods/
\-- hud_clock/
    |-- mod.manifest
    |-- Data/
    |   \-- hud_clock.pak
    \-- KCSE/
        \-- Plugins/
            |-- KCD2_HUDClock.dll
            |-- KCD2_HUDClock.ini
            \-- KCD2_HUDClock_License.txt
[/code]
[/spoiler]

[b]Without KCSE:[/b] also put an x64 [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases]Ultimate ASI Loader[/url] dinput8.dll beside WHGame.dll, and extract the optional [b]ASI add-on[/b] into the game folder. The ASI reads the KCD2_HUDClock.ini beside it.
[spoiler]
[code]
<KC:D 2 folder>/
\-- Bin/
    \-- Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/
        |-- dinput8.dll
        |-- KCD2_HUDClock.asi
        |-- KCD2_HUDClock.ini
        \-- KCD2_HUDClock_License.txt
[/code]
On Linux or Steam Deck, add [b]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dinput8=n,b" %command%[/b] to the Steam launch options.
[/spoiler]

With both KCSE and the add-on installed, only one copy runs. To uninstall, delete Mods/hud_clock and the add-on's files.

[size=5]Compatibility[/size]
[list]
[*]Works beside keybind, compass and other HUD mods.[/*]
[*]If a game update breaks it, the mod turns itself off.[/*]
[/list]
