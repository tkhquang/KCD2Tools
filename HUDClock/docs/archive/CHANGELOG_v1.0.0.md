## Initial Release

- Shows the game time on the HUD, and hides with it in menus, dialogue and cutscenes.
- Also shows in minigames (alchemy, smithing, dice, reading), and optionally in photo mode.
- 24 or 12 hours, part of the day, day count, and your computer's time and date.
- Sun and moon dial from the game's wait screen, with the time under it.
- Up to four lines, each with its own font, size, color and position.
- Always on, or for a few seconds on a key press or every few game hours. Keys can need a hold, and hide their controller button from the game.
- Replaces no game files and binds no keys by default.
- Settings apply while the game runs.
