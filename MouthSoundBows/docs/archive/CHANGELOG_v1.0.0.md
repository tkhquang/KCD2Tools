## Initial Release

- Every bow shot goes "Chiuuu!", made with a human mouth, right as the arrow leaves the bow.
- Crossbow shots go "Chiuuu!" too, a little deeper.
- Works for every shooter: Henry, bandits and hunters alike.
- Each shot varies a little in pitch and volume, so no two sound the same.
- One setting per weapon keeps the game's own shot audible under the Chiuuu.
