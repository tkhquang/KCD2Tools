[size=5]Foreword[/size]
Draw, aim, release, and instead of the bowstring you hear a grown man going:

[quote]Chiuuu![/quote]

Crossbows too, a little deeper. Henry, bandits, hunters: every shooter in Bohemia now makes their own sound effects, with their mouth.

This is a small joke mod and a showcase for [b]Sound Framework[/b]: one pack INI and two sound files, with no code and no paks. It shows how a pack can replace the game's own sounds with brand-new ones, timed to the moment the arrow leaves the bow.

[youtube]NwKRRVTob6I[/youtube]

[size=5]Key Features[/size]
[list]
[*]Every bow shot plays one of two mouth-made "Chiuuu!" takes, right as the arrow leaves the bow[/*]
[*]Crossbow shots get the same takes, a little deeper[/*]
[*]Each shot varies a little in pitch and volume, so no two sound the same[/*]
[*]Works for every shooter, Henry and NPCs alike[/*]
[*]Only the shot itself changes: nocking, reloading and hits keep the game's own sounds[/*]
[*]Optional: keep the game's own shot under the Chiuuu (see Options)[/*]
[*]Works with any game language[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
Install [b]Sound Framework[/b] first. Then install this mod with your mod manager, or extract the archive into your [b]Mods[/b] folder (create it if it is missing):
[code]
Mods/
\-- mouth_sound_bows/
    |-- mod.manifest
    \-- SoundFramework/
        |-- mouth_sound_bows.ini       (the pack)
        |-- mouth_sound_bows_01.ogg    (long take)
        \-- mouth_sound_bows_02.ogg    (short take)
[/code]
To check it, open [b]KCD2_SoundFramework.log[/b] after loading a save: it lists mouth_sound_bows.ini. Then shoot.

To uninstall, delete Mods/mouth_sound_bows.

[size=5]Options[/size]
To hear the game's own bowstring and crossbow under the Chiuuu, open [b]SoundFramework/mouth_sound_bows.ini[/b] and set [b]KeepOriginal = true[/b] in the section you want ([b][mouth_sound_bows_shot][/b] for bows, [b][mouth_sound_bows_crossbow][/b] for crossbows). Saving the file applies it while you play.

[size=5]Make Your Own[/size]
[b]mouth_sound_bows.ini[/b] is a complete, commented example of sound replacement: swap the files for your own takes, or point Replaces at other game sounds. The [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/blob/main/SoundFramework/docs/MODDING.md]Sound Framework modding guide[/url] covers every key and how to find the game's sound events with the EventLog debug option.
