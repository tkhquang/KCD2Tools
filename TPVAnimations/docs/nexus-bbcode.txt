[size=5]Foreword[/size]
Third-person animations for Henry, so he no longer stands like a statue. In first person the game's own animations play.

[size=5]Key Features[/size]
[list]
[*][b]Idle gestures:[/b] standing still, Henry stretches, scratches, yawns, spits, looks around and shifts his weight. Moving, crouching or turning stops the gesture at once.[/*]
[*][b]Pick your gestures:[/b] each gesture is its own file. Watch them on the [url=https://tkhquang.github.io/KCD2Tools/tpv_animations/]preview page[/url] and delete the ones you don't want.[/*]
[*][b]Drunk idle[/b] for a drunk Henry.[/*]
[*][b]Torch sprint:[/b] Henry holds the torch low in front of him instead of up.[/*]
[*][b]Smooth turns:[/b] turn the camera further while Henry turns on the spot, and he carries on turning instead of restarting his step or standing still.[/*]
[*][b]Door exit:[/b] holding a move key while Henry opens a door or gate no longer makes him slide when he walks off.[/*]
[*][url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/tree/main/TPVAnimations]Open source[/url], built on [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3981]Animation Framework[/url] (built with [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit]DetourModKit[/url])[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Requirements[/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3981]Animation Framework[/url] 1.0.0 or newer[/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3263]Proper Third Person View (TPV Camera)[/url] 3.0.0 or newer, or another camera mod that sets [b]mod_camera_view[/b][/*]
[*]Smooth turns need TPV Camera 3.2.1 or newer. With an older version, delete [b]tpv_animations_smooth_turn.pak[/b], or Henry's big turns restart mid-step.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
Install with your mod manager, or extract into your [b]Mods[/b] folder. To uninstall, delete [b]Mods/tpv_animations[/b].
[spoiler]
[code]
Mods/
\-- tpv_animations/
    |-- mod.manifest
    \-- Data/
        |-- tpv_animations_idle.pak
        |-- tpv_animations_idle_<gesture>.pak   (14 files)
        |-- tpv_animations_drunk.pak
        |-- tpv_animations_torch_sprint.pak
        |-- tpv_animations_smooth_turn.pak
        \-- tpv_animations_door_exit.pak
[/code]
[/spoiler]

[size=5]Choosing What Plays[/size]
Each part is its own pak in [b]Mods/tpv_animations/Data[/b]. Delete one to remove that part. The [url=https://tkhquang.github.io/KCD2Tools/tpv_animations/]preview page[/url] plays every animation, with its pak, and shows the game's animation next to the ones this mod replaces.
[list]
[*][b]tpv_animations_idle.pak[/b] - the calm idle, needed for any gesture[/*]
[*][b]tpv_animations_idle_<gesture>.pak[/b] - one gesture each[/*]
[*][b]tpv_animations_drunk.pak[/b] - drunk idle[/*]
[*][b]tpv_animations_torch_sprint.pak[/b] - torch sprint[/*]
[*][b]tpv_animations_smooth_turn.pak[/b] - smooth turns[/*]
[*][b]tpv_animations_door_exit.pak[/b] - door exit[/*]
[/list]
[spoiler]
Gestures: neck_stretch, back_stretch, scratch_face, forehead_wipe, yawn, spit, look_around, weight_shift_left, weight_shift_right, fidget02, fidget03, fidget04, fidget05, fidget07.
[/spoiler]

[size=5]Troubleshooting[/size]
Nothing changes in third person? Check that [b]KCD2_AnimationFramework.log[/b] exists beside WHGame.dll. Please [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/issues]report issues[/url] with that log and your mod list.
