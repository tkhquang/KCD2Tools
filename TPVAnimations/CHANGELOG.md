# Changelog

All notable changes to the TPVAnimations mod will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - Initial Release

- Idle gestures in third person: standing still, Henry stretches, scratches, yawns, spits, looks around and shifts his weight.
- Each gesture is its own pak: delete the ones you don't want.
- Drunk idle.
- Torch sprint with the torch held low in front.
