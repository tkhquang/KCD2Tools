## Lip Sync for Mod Cutscenes

- New: mods' own cutscenes can move the speakers' mouths with their voice lines, in every language.
- New: `sfw_cutscene_actors` console command names who speaks a cutscene line, for mods without a sound pack.
- Fixed: voice lines without mouth animation now move the speaker's mouth in English too. This covers mods' lines and about 3,600 of the game's own, mostly combat barks, shopkeepers and gossip.
