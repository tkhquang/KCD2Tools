[size=5]Foreword[/size]
[b]Sound Framework[/b] lets mods add sounds and voice lines to the game without writing code. A mod drops an INI file into its own SoundFramework folder; the framework plays its sounds on the game's own audio mixer, makes NPCs speak its voice lines with subtitles and lip movement, and can replace the game's sounds and voice lines with the mod's.

On its own it changes nothing you can hear: install it for the mods that need it. [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3862]Stop Right There, Criminal Scum[/url] and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3861]Chiuuu! Mouth-Sound Bows[/url] are two, and the [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/blob/main/SoundFramework/docs/MODDING.md]modding guide[/url] shows how to make your own.

Questions, packs to share or ideas? Join the [url=https://discord.gg/wzhhsQRnkZ]KCD Modding Community[/url] on Discord.

It is still early days. If something doesn't work properly, please report it with your game/mod versions, settings and log. Thank you for giving it a try!

[size=5]Key Features[/size]
[list]
[*][b]Sounds:[/b] play a pack's files on the game's buses (world, voice, music and others), or the game's own sound events at a new pitch and volume, in 3D or flat, with volume and pitch variation, loops, cooldowns and instance limits[/*]
[*][b]Sound replacement:[/b] play a pack sound in place of a game sound event or on top of it, everywhere or for chosen characters and animals, at its start or at a later moment such as a bow's release[/*]
[*][b]Voice lines:[/b] NPCs speak a pack's lines through the game's own speech system, with a bottom subtitle and speaker name, a chat bubble, or both[/*]
[*][b]Voice replacement:[/b] play a pack line instead of a game voice line, by exact key or by pattern[/*]
[*][b]Lip sync:[/b] a pack's lines move the speaker's mouth, with a small model that learned from the game's own animation or from the loudness of the voice. A voice dub without mouth animation of its own gets it on every game line too, cutscenes included[/*]
[*][b]Moods:[/b] facial expressions held while a line plays, with eleven defaults that packs can change or add to[/*]
[*][b]Live reload:[/b] saving a pack or the settings INI applies it while you play[/*]
[*]Every problem in a pack is logged with its file and line; debug options log the game's sound events and voice lines[/*]
[*]Ships as an ASI and as a KCSE plugin[/*]
[*][url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools]Open-source[/url], built on the [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/DetourModKit]DetourModKit[/url] modding framework[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Requirements[/size]
[list]
[*]Kingdom Come: Deliverance II on PC; built around Steam 1.5.6, with game-code signatures that also match the GOG and Game Pass builds[/*]
[*]For the ASI build: an ASI loader - [b]not included[/b] (see below)[/*]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
The framework comes in two builds in the Files tab. [b]Install one of them, not both.[/b] If both are installed, the second one to load stays off and says so in its log.

[size=4]Option A: ASI build[/size]
Open your KC:D 2 installation folder, then open the Bin/Win64MasterMaster... subfolder that contains [b]WHGame.dll[/b]. On Steam this is:
[code]<KC:D 2 installation folder>/Bin/Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/[/code]
Other stores use their own Bin/Win64MasterMaster... folder; use WHGame.dll to identify the correct one.

[b]Step 1: Install an ASI loader.[/b] If you already have a working ASI loader for KC:D 2, skip this step. Download [b]one x64[/b] variant of [url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases]Ultimate ASI Loader[/url] by ThirteenAG and extract its DLL beside WHGame.dll:
[list]
[*][url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/dinput8-x64.zip][b]dinput8.dll[/b][/url] - Usual choice if dinput8.dll is unused.[/*]
[*][url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/version-x64.zip][b]version.dll[/b][/url] - Alternative when dinput8.dll is already used.[/*]
[*][url=https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader/releases/download/x64-latest/winmm-x64.zip][b]winmm.dll[/b][/url] - Another alternative if version.dll is also used or does not load.[/*]
[/list]
KCSE also loads .asi files beside its dinput8.dll, so KCSE users need no separate loader. Do not overwrite a DLL belonging to another mod.

[b]Step 2: Install the mod.[/b] Extract the archive directly into the same folder, beside WHGame.dll, without an extra archive-named subfolder:
[code]
Bin/Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO/
|-- WHGame.dll                             (game file, already present)
|-- dinput8.dll                            (ASI loader)
|-- KCD2_SoundFramework.asi                (this mod)
|-- KCD2_SoundFramework.ini                (settings)
|-- KCD2_SoundFramework_moods.ini          (default facial moods)
|-- KCD2_SoundFramework_lipmodel.bin       (advanced lip sync model)
\-- KCD2_SoundFramework.log                (created when the mod loads)
[/code]
On Wine/Proton, add an override for your loader in Steam's [b]Properties -> Launch Options[/b]:
[code]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dinput8=n,b" %command%[/code]
Use version=n,b or winmm=n,b instead when using that loader name.

[size=4]Option B: KCSE build[/size]
Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3332]KCSE[/url], then install the KCSE archive with your mod manager, or extract it into your [b]Mods[/b] folder (create it if it is missing):
[code]
Mods/
\-- soundframework/
    |-- mod.manifest
    \-- KCSE/
        \-- Plugins/
            |-- KCD2_SoundFramework.dll
            |-- KCD2_SoundFramework.ini
            |-- KCD2_SoundFramework_moods.ini
            \-- KCD2_SoundFramework_lipmodel.bin
[/code]

[size=4]Verify[/size]
Load a save, then open [b]KCD2_SoundFramework.log[/b] beside the mod's DLL or ASI. It lists every sound pack the framework found and any problem in them.

When updating, back up your customized INI before extracting the new archive, then reapply your settings to the supplied INI.

[size=5]Configuration[/size]
Open [b]KCD2_SoundFramework.ini[/b] beside the mod with a text editor such as Notepad. Save the file to apply changes while playing; only [b][LipSync] Enabled[/b] needs a restart.
[list]
[*][b][Settings] LogLevel[/b] - TRACE, DEBUG, INFO, WARNING or ERROR.[/*]
[*][b][LipSync] Enabled[/b] - Lip movement for voice lines without the game's own lip-sync animation, such as custom and replaced lines.[/*]
[*][b][LipSync] Mode = ADVANCED[/b] (default) - A small machine-learned model moves the whole lower face: jaw, lips, mouth corners and smile lines. It needs [b]KCD2_SoundFramework_lipmodel.bin[/b] beside the INI.[/*]
[*][b][LipSync] Mode = BASIC[/b] - Jaw and lip shapes from the voice's loudness and spectrum. This mode is also the fallback when the model file is missing.[/*]
[*][b][LipSync] OffsetMs[/b] - Moves the mouth earlier (positive) or later (negative) than the sound. Try values live with [b]sfw_lipsync_offset <ms>[/b] in the console.[/*]
[*][b][LipSync] DubbedVoices = AUTO[/b] (default) - Voice dubs: the game shows English mouth animation for a voice language without its own. The lips then follow each line's own voice, in cutscenes too, and the animation's smiles and frowns stay. [b]ON[/b] always does this, [b]OFF[/b] never.[/*]
[*][b][Debug] EventLog / SpeakLog[/b] - For mod authors: log the game's sound events and voice lines, to find what to replace or reuse.[/*]
[/list]
[b]KCD2_SoundFramework_moods.ini[/b] holds the default moods. It is replaced on update; change a mood by defining it again in your own pack.

[size=5]Console Commands[/size]
Open the console with [b]~[/b].
[list]
[*][b]sfw_play <sound> [x y z][/b] - Plays a pack sound at a world position, or at the camera.[/*]
[*][b]sfw_stop <sound>[/b] / [b]sfw_stop_all[/b] - Stops a sound / every framework sound.[/*]
[*][b]sfw_say <entity> <line or voice path>[/b] - An NPC says a pack voice line, or speaks a recording.[/*]
[*][b]sfw_lipsync_offset <ms>[/b] - Moves the mouth earlier or later.[/*]
[*][b]sfw_mood_override <mood> [pose:weight ...][/b] / [b]sfw_mood_dump <mood>[/b] - Tries mood values live / writes a mood to the log.[/*]
[*][b]sfw_reload[/b] / [b]sfw_list[/b] - Reads every pack again / writes the catalog to the log.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Quick Test[/size]
No pack needed. Load a save, open the console and make Henry say other characters' lines in different moods:
[code]
sfw_say Dude Localization/dialog/trosecko/konecZabomysichValek/jmil_comb_bojovnici_ty_odporny_RkVq mood:angry strength:2 You disgusting, slimy, rotten piece of shit!
sfw_say Dude Localization/dialog/open_world/lazne_a_lazebnice/aals_obje_lazebnice_sss__jeste_AjPM mood:happy Wonderful! You'll see, you'll be happy to come back here.
sfw_say Dude Localization/dialog/open_world/crime_reaction_barks/mrtvola/mbis_npc__npc_vidi__promin_str_0DUg mood:sad I'm sorry, but I can't stand to be here any longer.
sfw_say Dude Localization/dialog/kutnohorsko/pogrom/tsho_zena_pogrom_ob_na_pomoc_S0g7 mood:scared Somebody help us!
sfw_say Dude Localization/dialog/open_world/lazne_a_lazebnice/aals_obje_lazebnice_ale_ty_uz__wAcW mood:surprised You're already undressed? That's a new one!
sfw_say Dude Localization/dialog/trosecko/konecZabomysichValek/jmil_comb_bojovnici_olbramovi__xiB6 mood:disgusted Olbram's breath smells like a bull's arse!
sfw_say Dude Localization/dialog/utils/roomrenting/lcar_zena_hospodska_to_pujde_z_8Lbz mood:smug Very well, but you'll have to pay in advance.
[/code]
[list]
[*][b]Dude[/b] is Henry. A third-person camera mod shows his face.[/*]
[*][b]strength[/b] scales the mood from 0 to 2 (default 1). The text at the end is the subtitle.[/*]
[*]None of these recordings has the game's lip-sync animation, and the voices are other characters', so the mouth movement is the framework's lip sync and the mood moves the whole face.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]For Mod Authors[/size]
A sound pack is an ordinary mod folder with a [b]SoundFramework[/b] folder inside; every .ini file in it is a pack:
[code]
[mymod_bell]
Files = bell_1.ogg, bell_2.ogg
Bus = world
Replaces = event:/animals/dog/dog_bark

[mymod_guard_hello]
Voice = Localization/dialog/mymod/mymd_hello_01
TextKey = mymd_hello_01
Speaker = soul_ui_name_guard
Display = subtitle
Mood = angry
ReplacesVoice = zaty_strazny_z_*
[/code]
The [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/blob/main/SoundFramework/docs/MODDING.md]modding guide[/url] covers every key, the bus names, voice recordings and text tables, moods, and how to find the game's event paths and line keys. [b]Stop Right There, Criminal Scum[/b] is a complete example pack.

Please list Sound Framework as a requirement on your mod page. Share your packs and ask for help in the [url=https://discord.gg/wzhhsQRnkZ]KCD Modding Community[/url] Discord.

[size=5]Known Limitations[/size]
[list]
[*]Voice recordings and text live in the mod's paks, which the game reads only at startup; changing them needs a restart. Pack INIs and loose sound files reload live.[/*]
[*]Game lines that have Warhorse's own lip-sync animation keep it: a mood then moves only the upper face.[/*]
[*]Game updates may require a mod update if the framework stops finding the game code it hooks.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Troubleshooting[/size]
[list]
[*]Set LogLevel = DEBUG under [Settings], save the INI and reproduce the problem.[/*]
[*]Check KCD2_SoundFramework.log beside the mod. Pack problems are listed with their file and line.[/*]
[*]If a mod's sounds don't play, check that the mod has a SoundFramework folder with its INI and that the log lists the pack.[/*]
[/list]
Please [url=https://github.com/tkhquang/KCD2Tools/issues]report issues[/url] with the log, INI, game/mod versions and reproduction steps.

[b]About advanced lip sync:[/b] it is machine-learned. A small neural network (about 680,000 parameters), trained on Kingdom Come: Deliverance II's own voice lines and facial animation, turns each voice line into mouth movement while you play. It generates facial animation only: no audio, text or images. Set [b]Mode = BASIC[/b] for the mouth without machine learning.
